# Demo audio

`Hum.wav` and `Click.wav` are original, generated PCM16 mono sounds produced by
`Scripts/GenerateAudioFixtures.py` using the Python standard library. They contain
no recordings or third-party samples and are available under the repository's terms.

Regenerate them with `python Scripts/GenerateAudioFixtures.py`.

Open `Content/Scenes/AudioDemo.scene`, press Play, and use WASD/arrows to move the
listener toward or away from the source. Press Space to play overlapping clicks.
